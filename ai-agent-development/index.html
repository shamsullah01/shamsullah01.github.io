Frequently asked questions

What does an AI engineer like you actually build?

AI agents and chatbots that work on top of large language models, trained on a business's own information, plus the full-stack software around them: the chat interface, the API, integrations with calendars and CRMs, and deployment.

Can the AI agent book appointments?

Yes. On Agentra by Valour AI, agents check availability and book appointments directly into the business's calendar, alongside answering questions and capturing leads.

Will the agent make things up?

The agent is grounded in your own documents and set up to say it doesn't know, or hand off to a person, when a question is outside that material.

Can you add AI to software we already have?

Yes. As a full-stack developer I can add AI features to an existing React/Node.js or NestJS application, for example AI data processing like the IMS platform, where it reduced manual entry by about 30%.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. I work remotely across time zones and am open to freelance, contract and full-time roles.
